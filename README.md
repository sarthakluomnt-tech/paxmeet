Building Paxmeet (paxmeet.com) — A location-first platform for real-world events, interest circles, and local community discovery.
### About Me & Projects

I am a developer and digital marketing professional building scalable web architectures and conversion-focused digital platforms. 

Currently, I am leading the engineering and development for **[Paxmeet](https://www.paxmeet.com/)**—a location-first social discovery platform designed to connect people through real-world micro-events and interest circles.

---

### Core Engineering Focus: Paxmeet

Paxmeet bridges the gap between digital interaction and real-world offline engagement by optimizing spatial discovery and trust verification.

* **Geospatial Engine:** Utilizing spatial indexing (Redis Geo / PostGIS) to execute high-speed radius queries for local circles and events.
* **Trust & Verification Pipeline:** Implementing secure identity verification flows to maintain safe, authenticated host-to-participant interactions.
* **Event Synchronization:** Real-time state management ensuring RSVP status and event data update seamlessly across clients.
